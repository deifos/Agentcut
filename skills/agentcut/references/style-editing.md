# Style editing workflow

A selected style supplies visual defaults and an editing guide. Selection alone does not clean speech, find footage, improve source resolution or mix audio. Carry out these steps when the user sends an editing request.

1. Inspect the source and the current timeline. Read the selected entry in .agentcut/components/STYLES.md. Preserve user edits. Record a short cue sheet in notes.md.
2. Transcribe with agentcut_transcribe. Review timed words and listen to candidate cuts. Remove verified repeated takes, unnecessary silence and filler words only where the sentence still sounds natural. Keep meaningful pauses and breaths. If timing or listening is unavailable, do not guess the cuts; report the limit.
3. Assemble speech first. Split and trim source clips, close gaps, and keep source files unchanged. Rebuild caption timings against the edited sequence. Move related graphics, B-roll and audio with their speech. Check every join for lost syllables and audio clicks.
4. Review framing about every 4–6 seconds. Choose a new crop, wider reset or relevant B-roll at a change of idea, example or emphasis. This is a review interval, not a fixed cut interval. Preserve face and eye lines in each variant. Use supported clip boxes or an explicit scene transform; do not invent a zoom property. Keep camera:none on clips if a style camera would add an unwanted repeating move. The four new styles use a still camera by default.
5. Add supplied or approved B-roll to explain the words. Use source labels on evidence. If needed footage is missing, use a useful card or diagram and report the missing asset. Do not invent quotes, statistics or product details.
6. Apply the palette, type and restrained grade. Check skin tones, highlight clipping and text contrast. A filter cannot restore focus or upscale source detail. Keep music below speech, use sparse sounds on visible cues, and follow references/sound.md for measurement. Apply EQ or compression only through supported tools or a documented audio-processing step; a preset name does not apply them.
7. Capture entrances, holds and exits in each size. Use a contact sheet for pacing. Listen to the final mix. Check caption sync after all cuts. Report what changed and any source or tool limits.

## Research behind the four directions

These are original Agentcut styles inspired by editing principles. They are not creator replicas, endorsements or a ranking of current trends. Palettes, layouts and preview choreography are our design choices.

- Signal: direct business advice, evidence and a next action. Alex Hormozi's June 2024 note favors useful business content and less distracting production: https://www.acquisition.com/mozi-money-minute/i-made-a-youtube-video . His official workbook describes hook, retain and reward: https://shop.acquisition.com/products/workbook-3-pack-digital . This supports a clear message, not constant animated captions.
- Studyroom: lesson assembly, relevant supporting visuals, then sound and color polish. Ali Abdaal explains this three-stage workflow: https://aliabdaal.com/youtube/the-ultimate-guide-to-youtube/ . Preserve useful pauses rather than applying blanket silence removal.
- Afterglow: a product story with detail footage, a measured grade and music-led scene changes. Daniel Schiffer's own cooking B-roll editing breakdown is a visual reference: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qSMi0ygL--E . Actual footage and timeline edits are required; a look preset alone does not create that sequence.
- Fieldnote: documentary setup, question and resolution with traceable evidence. Colin and Samir discuss audience, story planning and a three-act structure in their first-person interview: https://www.musicbed.com/articles/filmmaking/youtube/the-biggest-mistake-filmmakers-make-with-colin-and-samir/ . Serif type and rust source markers are Agentcut design choices.
